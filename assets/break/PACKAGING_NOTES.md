# Break — Italian curriculum package

Self-initiated **packaging-only** sample project based on the supplied brief.

The main product is a high school Italian curriculum set. The design proposes a warm grey paperboard box with a minimal typographic front, spine and back. There are no photographs or illustrations on the package. Oversized **ITALIAN** makes the contents clear, while a small chartreuse line brings energy without overpowering the grey.

The line **“Here for every lesson.”** reflects the company's customer-service focus.

## Material direction

- Proposed uncoated biodegradable paperboard
- Water-based ink
- No plastic lamination

Biodegradability and print specifications require supplier verification before production.
