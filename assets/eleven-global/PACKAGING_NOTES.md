# Eleven Global — apartment presentation box

Self-initiated **packaging-only** sample project by Vishal Kumar, based on the supplied brief.

The concept is a rigid presentation box for a pre-construction apartment offer. The apartment illustration occupies the largest area on the front, and **YOUR NEXT CHAPTER.** is the dominant title. Blue communicates importance and trust; a warm accent makes the package feel lively.

## Package artwork

- `kit-mockup.svg` — closed-box presentation mockup
- `box-front.svg` — flat front-panel artwork
- `box-back.svg` — flat back-panel artwork
- `residences.svg` — source illustration used on the package

The architecture is illustrative and does not represent a verified development. Final dielines, dimensions, legal copy and print specifications require project and print-vendor details.
