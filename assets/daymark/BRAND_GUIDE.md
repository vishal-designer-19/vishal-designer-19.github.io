# DAYMARK — sample jacket brand kit

Self-initiated portfolio concept by Vishal Kumar for an accessible adult outerwear brand.

## Brand idea

DAYMARK makes the jacket you reach for every day feel considered, useful and distinctive.

- Name: **DAYMARK**
- Positioning: accessible outerwear with thoughtful utility details
- Audience: adults looking for easy, confident everyday layers
- Tagline: **Every day has a direction.**
- Campaign line: **Own the outside.**
- Voice: brief, assured, useful and direct

## Logo

- `logo-lockup-light.svg`: primary lockup on deep ink or another dark ground
- `logo-mark.svg`: dark symbol for light grounds, woven labels and small applications
- `logo-mark-light.svg`: light symbol for dark grounds
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the width of its coral square.
- Minimum mark size: 20 px digital or 7 mm print.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolour or add effects to the logo.

## Colour

| Colour | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Ink | `#172C35` | Primary type, jacket body, logo and large fields |
| Warm Stone | `#F4EFE5` | Jacket panels, mailer and neutral ground |
| Signal Coral | `#FF664F` | Signature accent, zipper pull and callout |
| Storm Blue | `#AFC5D2` | Supporting outerwear tone and pattern ground |

Use Deep Ink and Warm Stone as the base. Keep Signal Coral small enough to feel intentional.

## Typography

- Headlines: Poppins, bold to extra bold
- Product details and body: Poppins, regular to bold
- Keep headlines short and direct. Use generous space around the wordmark and mark.

## Applications

- `pattern.svg`: repeat graphic for garment lining, mailers and digital backgrounds
- `jacket-hero.svg`: illustrative utility jacket concept
- `packaging.svg`: hangtag and mailer concept
- `campaign.svg`: launch poster concept
