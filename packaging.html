Apartment presentation box

FRONT / SIDE / BACK01 / 03

The packaging idea

A major decision,
made tangible.

A rigid presentation box gives the apartment offer a physical presence for couples considering a future home. The front gives the building the largest area, while the oversized “Your Next Chapter” title creates a clear first impression.

Global Blue conveys importance and trust. A warm highlight and open sky keep the package lively and optimistic.

The artwork

One box. Every side considered.

Flat front and back artwork support the presentation mockup. The front shows the product prominently; the back keeps the message simple and leaves room for real project details when supplied.

Packaging only

A new home, presented well.

This is a self-initiated sample packaging project. The apartment image is illustrative; no property features, prices or availability are claimed.

12 / Sample project

Break.

Minimal packaging for a high school Italian curriculum, designed for millennial buyers. A warm grey paperboard box uses type alone to feel calm, clear and a little lively.

Simple by design.

The box holds the curriculum set. Large “Italian” type makes the subject instantly clear, while a quiet support line reflects Break’s customer-service focus.

Material direction.

Proposed uncoated biodegradable paperboard, water-based ink and no plastic lamination. Final material claims depend on supplier verification.
